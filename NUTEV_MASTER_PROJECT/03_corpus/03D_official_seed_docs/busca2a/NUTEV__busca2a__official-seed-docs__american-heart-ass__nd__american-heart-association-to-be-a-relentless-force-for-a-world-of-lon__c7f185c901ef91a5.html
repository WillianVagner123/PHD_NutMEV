What is Heart Walk?
The Heart Walk is the American Heart Association's premier event for raising funds to save lives from this country's No. 1 and No. 5 killers - heart disease and stroke.

Celebrating Survivors
Heart disease and stroke survivors are an important part of our Heart Walks. Each survivor receives a red ball cap and have the opportunity to gather with other survivors.

What’s a National Team?
National Company Teams are companies that have committed to participate in the Heart Walk across their organizations and set a company-wide fundraising goal!
